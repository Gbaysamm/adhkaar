How it works

Set it once. It keeps you on time.

Everything follows your masjid's salah times, so the adhkaar come when you've just prayed and your phone is in your hand.

1

Set your masjid's times

Start from common local times and tap any of them to match your masjid. Your times replace the defaults.

2

The adhkaar open after salah

Around 6:00 AM, after Fajr is prayed, and 4:45 PM, after Asr. Tap to count; each moves on by itself.

3

Finish, and keep the streak

Every finished session counts toward your streak and your month, so you can see the habit grow.

Three modes

As gentle or as firm as you need.

Salah reminders

A reminder you won't scroll past.

At each salah time you set, a card comes up over whatever app is open, even on the lock screen, with a chime and a vibration you feel in your pocket.

Three small steps

Wudu, the masjid, phone down: what getting ready means right now.

The current salah stays lit

On Today, a salah shows as "Now" for half an hour, through the congregation.

الصَّلَاةُ عَلَى وَقْتِهَا

The most beloved deed to Allah: salah at its proper time.

Sahih al-Bukhari 527
Busy? Take a break

A short pause, never a skip.

Pause for 10, 20, 30 or 60 minutes, only as long as there's still time today. Before it pauses, the app asks once more, gently.

How little is left

"Just 17 adhkaar left. A few minutes, and today's adhkaar are done."

What you've built

"You have kept them for 22 days in a row. Don't let today be the gap."

Hold to pause

Pausing takes a five-second press and hold. Let go, and you're back to reading.

Everything for the day

More than morning and evening.

Adhkaar for every moment

After each salah, before sleep, on waking, and the everyday duas, each with its own reminder. Add your own duas, keep favourites and listen to the recitation.

Your progress

Streaks, the month at a glance and which weekdays slip.

By the moon

Hijri and Gregorian side by side. In Nigeria it follows the moon sightings announced by the NSCIA.

A reminder for today

One verse or hadith each day from 1,231, shown exactly as published, ready to share as a card.

Seven widgets

The next adhkaar, the Hijri date, your streak and more, each in three styles.

Help, inside the app

A plain guide to the times and modes, a test kit that tries every alert in ten seconds, and a contact form that sends your phone's details with your message.
